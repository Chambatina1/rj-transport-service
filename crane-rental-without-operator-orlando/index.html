FAQ

Questions

Can I rent the crane without an operator?

Yes, when your own crew is trained to run it. Call (407) 595-9726 and dispatch will explain the requirements.

What is a bare or dry crane rental?

It means you rent only the crane, without an R&J operator, and your own trained crew runs it.

Can I switch to a rental with operator?

Yes. If you'd rather have an experienced R&J operator run it, ask dispatch for a crane rental with operator.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how long you need it and the location. Call or send a request and we'll give you the price first.
