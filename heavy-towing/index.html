When you call

Have this ready for dispatch

The more we know up front, the faster we send the right truck:

  • Exact location — highway, mile marker or cross street
  • Vehicle type, and whether the trailer is loaded
  • What happened — breakdown, accident, stuck, off road
  • Where the vehicle needs to go
  • Fleet, insurance or PO information if applicable

FAQ

Heavy towing questions

What counts as heavy-duty towing?

Heavy-duty towing covers vehicles too large or heavy for a standard tow truck — typically Class 7 and 8 trucks such as tractor-trailers, dump trucks, buses, coaches, large RVs and construction equipment.

How fast can you get to me in Orlando?

Response time depends on where you are, traffic and the job. Call (407) 595-9726 with your location and we will give you an honest ETA before we roll.

Can you tow a loaded trailer?

In many cases, yes. Tell the dispatcher the load type and approximate weight so we send the right equipment. If the load needs to be transferred, we can help coordinate that too.

Do you tow long distance?

Yes. Besides Central Florida, we handle long-distance heavy towing and transport across Florida and into neighboring states.

Do you work with fleets and insurance companies?

Yes. We work with fleet managers, leasing companies and insurers. Call to set up your fleet so dispatch has your information on file.
