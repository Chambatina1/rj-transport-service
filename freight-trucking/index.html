FAQ

Freight questions

What kind of freight do you haul?

General and palletized freight, steel, metal sheet and building materials, intermodal containers (drayage), and mail and parcel freight. If you have something else, ask — we will tell you straight if it’s a fit.

Do you offer dedicated or contract capacity?

Yes. Dedicated lanes and recurring schedules are the core of our freight business. We build the schedule around your volume — weekly, several times a week, daily, or for the length of a project.

Which states do you cover?

All of Florida, neighboring states such as Georgia and Alabama, and lanes further north along the I-75 and I-95 corridors, including the Carolinas and Tennessee. Send us your lanes for a quote.

Are you a broker?

No. R&J Transport Service Inc is an asset-based carrier, USDOT #1328058, operating our own trucks out of Orlando since 2004.

How do I get a contract rate?

Send your lanes, average weekly volume and freight type through the quote form or call (407) 595-9726. We review and come back with a rate and a capacity plan.
